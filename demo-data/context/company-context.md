## Rules

1. **Tax set-aside.** 25% of the net amount (excluding VAT) of every client payment we receive is kept for income tax and social contributions. At the start of each month the previous month's set-aside is moved to the tax reserve account.
2. **VAT.** We charge 21% VAT and declare it quarterly by invoice date: the VAT on every invoice issued in the quarter is owed, paid or not. The Q3 payment (invoices dated 1 July to 30 September) is due on **25 October 2026**. For cash planning, use the VAT on our sales invoices; VAT we can deduct on purchases is small and the accountant confirms the final figure.
3. **Cash floor.** Never let the bank balance fall below **2 months of fixed costs**. Fixed costs are the recurring costs above, owner pay and contractor included, with annual plans divided by 12. A new fixed cost raises the floor from the month it starts.
4. **Receipts.** A bank line needs a receipt in `receipts/` when it is (a) a card payment of EUR 50 or more that is not a monthly subscription, or (b) any charge in a foreign currency. Monthly subscriptions, direct debits and transfers (contractor, suppliers paid against their invoice, tax office) are covered by documents the accountant already holds. Card payments under EUR 50 (coffee with clients, parking, taxis, transit, small supplies, postage) need no receipt.
5. **Big receivables.** Any open invoice over EUR 5,000 gets two scenarios in a cash forecast: collected by a stated date, and not collected.
6. **Approvals.** The AI reads, categorises, calculates and drafts. Sofia approves anything sent to a client or the accountant. Nobody but Sofia pays, transfers or files anything.
